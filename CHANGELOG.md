# v1.0.0

First release.

- Five games: Deathroll, High-Low, Coinflip, Blackjack and Texas Hold'em.
- Every result comes from the game server's own /roll, and every player's addon checks it.
- A lobby to open and join tables in your party or raid, a table view with seats, cards and dice, and an Immersive off mode that plays inside the lobby window.
- Chat play: friends without the addon can sit and play by typing in party or raid chat (join, leave, hit, stand, raise 5, ready, rules and so on).
- A ledger of who owes whom, marked paid automatically when you trade gold with that player.
- Practice tables with bots: /gf test.
